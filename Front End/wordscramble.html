How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty and start the game.
  2. Unscramble each Mati heritage word before the timer runs out.
  3. Submit your answer to earn the fixed score for that difficulty.

Each round has a different number of words and total time. Harder rounds use longer heritage names and phrases.
