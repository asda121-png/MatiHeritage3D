How to play

  1. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard to set the board size.
  2. Slide a tile into the empty space to move it.
  3. Arrange the tiles in order to restore the heritage photo before time runs out.

Each move slides one tile into the empty space. Solve the board before time runs out; larger grids offer a greater challenge.
