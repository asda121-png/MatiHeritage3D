How to play

  1. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard, then start the trivia round.
  2. Read each Mati heritage question and select one answer before the timer reaches zero.
  3. Earn a score for correct answers. Try to get the highest score you can.

Each difficulty has a different number of questions, time limit, and point value. Your total score is added to your Mati Heritage score when the round ends.
