Privacy

How Mati Heritage 3D handles information across the platform.

Privacy at Mati Heritage 3D

Mati Heritage 3D is designed to make local heritage accessible while respecting the privacy of its visitors and registered users. This policy explains what information the platform uses, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

What we collect

You can browse heritage sites, maps, galleries, and other public content without creating an account.

If you create an account, we may keep the information needed for authentication and your account profile. When you use interactive features, we may also record scores, progress, achievements, and leaderboard activity connected to your account.

Why we use it

We use information to:

  • provide account access and protect the platform;
  • save game progress, scores, and achievements;
  • display leaderboard results using your chosen profile name;
  • operate, maintain, and improve Mati Heritage 3D; and
  • respond to support and accessibility concerns.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Who we share it with

Information may be processed by the services that support authentication, data storage, hosting, and platform operation. These services are used only to provide Mati Heritage 3D features and support the project.

How long we keep it

Account information and connected activity are kept while your account is active or as needed to provide the platform. You may request assistance with reviewing or removing your information.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to review, correct, or remove information connected to your account. For questions about your account or personal information, contact the City Tourism Office through the Contact page.

Children

If you are under 18, please use account and interactive features with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the platform or its information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
