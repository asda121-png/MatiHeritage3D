Mati City history

A Walk through Time

From a Mandaya settlement on the east coast to the capital of Davao Oriental. Scroll to explore the milestones that shaped the city we document today.

1861

Origins

Settlement founded

Captain Prudencio Garcia and Juan Nazareno established Mati on the east coast. The name comes from the Mandaya word Maa-ti, referring to a creek said to dry quickly even after heavy rain.

1903

Municipality

Municipality established

On October 29, Mati became a regular municipality under Act No. 21, joining other east-coast towns under a formal local government system.

1967

Capital

Provincial capital

Mati was designated capital of Davao Oriental, strengthening its role as the province’s political and economic center on the Philippine Sea coast.

2003

Centennial

100 years as a municipality

Mati marked its centennial, a century since Act No. 21, celebrating growth from a coastal settlement into a thriving provincial hub.

2007

Cityhood

Republic Act No. 9408

The municipality was converted into a component city. Voters ratified the law in June, beginning Mati’s path toward full city status.

2011

Affirmed

Cityhood upheld

The Supreme Court upheld Mati’s cityhood on February 15. Mati City, the only city in Davao Oriental, celebrates its foundation day every June 19.

Today

Heritage

Living cultural landscape

Mati continues to grow as a tourism and cultural destination, from Sambuokan and Pujada festivals to protected bays, historic structures, and Mandaya heritage. Mati Heritage 3D documents this living identity for the community and the world.

Who we are

Our story

We built this platform so students, visitors, and communities can explore Mati’s cultural identity beyond textbooks and brochures.

The project

Mati Heritage 3D grew from a student-led capstone commitment to digitize Local Cultural Inventory sites across Mati City. Each heritage property is documented with photographs, descriptions, and coordinates aligned with the LGU cultural inventory.

The visitor portal brings those records together: an interactive map, 3D models, photo galleries, festival timelines, and educational games.
